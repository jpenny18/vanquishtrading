What is Vanquish Trading and how does it work?

Vanquish gives traders the ability to participate in the CFD markets without needing to risk thousands of dollars of their own capital. Instead of depositing your own trading funds, you select one of our trading plans and begin trading in a simulated environment, where your performance determines how far you progress.

You can start with an Evaluation. Prove consistency and risk management in a simulated account. Once you pass the evaluation, move to a sim funded account.

As you demonstrate consistency, discipline, and profitability in your simulated account, we transition you to a live brokerage account funded with our capital. From that point forward, you keep a share of the profits you generate.

Vanquish is designed to provide a low-cost, low-risk pathway into CFD trading, backed by clearly defined rules and transparent expectations.

You'll also gain access to:

  • Modern trading platforms and technology
  • Responsive, human support
  • An active Discord community filled with traders, coaches, and members of our team

Our goal is to give traders a safer, more accessible way to enter the CFD markets and build long-term trading success with guidance, structure, and real opportunity.

How quickly can i get funded?

With our real-time activations you can activate a passed evaluation within 10 minutes of passing the objectives. This is something other firms wish they had and our traders love it.

How many accounts can i have?

Currently, we allow 2 accounts per household.

Do I have to pay monthly for my accounts?

No. All of our accounts are a one-time fee. We do not have monthly subscriptions for our trading platform or accounts.
