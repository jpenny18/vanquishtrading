Questions?

What is Vanquish Trading and how does it work?

Vanquish Trading is a one-step CFD prop firm. You buy a challenge, reach a 10% profit target, and stay inside the loss rules. There is no minimum trading days, no time limit, and no consistency rule. When you pass, you keep 90% of the profits.

How quickly can i get funded?

There is no minimum number of trading days and no time limit. You can pass as soon as you hit the 10% profit target without breaking the 5% daily loss or 10% max loss rules.

How many accounts can i have?

Each challenge is its own account. You can purchase more than one size, and each one is paid for on its own.

Do I have to pay monthly for my accounts?

No. Every plan is a one-time fee. There is no monthly subscription and no account activation fee.
